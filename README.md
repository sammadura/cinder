# Cinder

A static night-warden yard. Keep a small hearth through eight watches. Night-things walk in from the dark to snuff the fire. You throw sparks. Last until pale dawn.

Open `index.html` in a browser. WASD or arrows move, mouse aims, click or hold throws. Space retries after a loss or a win. Esc or P pauses. M mutes. On a phone, the left stick moves and the right disc throws.

Credit: Tony Dinh's public Demo 2 post https://x.com/tdinh_me/status/2090788589143789599 and the live game at https://survive10waves.com. Cinder takes the same distribution shape -- one static URL, a wave-survival you open and play with no account, on-screen controls, and a restart -- and none of that product's name, writing, 3D assets, guns, jetpack, extraction pad, weapon rack, coins, sectors, music, or colors.
